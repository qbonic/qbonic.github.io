Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Effective Date: August 18, 2026 | Entity: VitanuGenAI Technology Pvt Ltd

1. 100% In-Browser Data Processing Guarantee

Qbonic processes all user CSVs, Excel files, and Google Sheets 100% locally in your device's web browser memory (RAM). Raw spreadsheet data is never uploaded, stored, or transmitted to any cloud servers or third-party databases.

2. Personal Data We Collect (Beta Form Submissions)

When you apply for Beta Access, we collect your Full Name, Work Email, Role, Country, Use Case, and Primary Frustration. This data is collected solely to manage early invitations and communicate beta rollout updates.

3. EU GDPR & US CCPA Rights

Under EU GDPR and California CCPA/CPRA, you have the right to request access to, rectification of, or deletion of your email data ("Right to be Forgotten") at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us at admin@qbonic.com.

4. Zero Third-Party Selling & Zero Tracking Cookies

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information. We do not use third-party cross-site tracking cookies.
