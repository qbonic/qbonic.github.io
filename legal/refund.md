## 2. 1-Click Self-Serve Cancellation Anytime
You can cancel your subscription or turn off automatic renewals at any time with 1 click directly inside the application:

**How to cancel:**
1. Log in to Qbonic and navigate to the **Plans Page**.
2. Click **"Turn Off Auto-Renew"** or **"Cancel Subscription"**.
3. Confirm your choice.

### Cancellation Scenarios:
- **Canceling during the 7-Day Free Trial:** Terminates immediately. Your card will never be charged a single penny.
- **Canceling after trial (Paid Subscription):** Turns off automatic renewal billing. You retain uninterrupted access to all Pro features until the end of your prepaid billing period (your expiry date). No future renewal payments will ever be charged.
